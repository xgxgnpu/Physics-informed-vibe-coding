# E5 — Characteristic Frequency (`w_char`) Sensitivity and Auto-Estimation

**Purpose.** Systematically characterize SV-SNN's sensitivity to the characteristic-frequency prior `w_char`: a ratio scan, multi-peak choice, FFT-based auto-estimation, noise robustness, and frozen vs learnable frequencies.

**Reviewer comments addressed.** R1.5, R3.2, R4.3, R5.1, R9.3.

## Setup

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Model | Separable SV-SNN, M = 6, K = 32, 6000 epochs, Adam (lr = 1e-3) |
| True frequency | κ = 24π ≈ 75.4; multi-peak `k₁ = 12π ≈ 37.7`, `k₂ = 24π ≈ 75.4` |
| Sub-experiments | (a) ratio scan; (b) multi-peak `w_char`; (c) chirp frozen vs learnable; (d) FFT auto; (e) wrong prior 0.6κ; (f) noisy `w_char` |
| Seeds | 3 (best L2, mean ± std) |

## Key results

(a) `w_char = ratio · κ` scan — best L2:

| ratio | 0.50 | 0.75 | 0.90 | 1.00 | 1.10 | 1.25 | 1.50 | 2.00 |
|-------|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|
| L2 | 0.726 | 0.092 | 0.082 | 0.036 | 0.039 | 0.024 | 0.016 | 0.328 |

(b) Multi-peak `w_char` choice — best L2: low `k₁` = 0.476; mean = 0.093; **high `k₂` = 0.024**; split = 0.063.

(d) FFT auto-estimation: true κ = 75.40, FFT estimate = 75.40 (0.0% error), best L2 = 0.0360 — identical to manual.

(f) Noisy prior `w_char = κ·(1 + ε·N(0,1))` — best L2:

| ε | 0.00 | 0.05 | 0.10 | 0.20 | 0.30 |
|---|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|
| L2 | 0.036 | 0.041 | 0.029 | 0.018 | 0.042 |

(c)(e) Frozen vs learnable frequencies:

| Case | Frozen | Learnable |
|------|-------:|----------:|
| (c) spatial chirp | **0.0037 ± 0.0009** | 0.0090 ± 0.0020 |
| (e) wrong prior 0.6κ | **0.113 ± 0.040** | 0.283 ± 0.008 |

## Conclusions

- Sensitivity is clearly **U-shaped**: severe underestimation (0.5κ → 0.726) is catastrophic, while a robust plateau exists at ratio 1.0–1.5 (L2 0.016–0.036).
- For multi-peak problems, set `w_char` to the **highest** significant frequency; the low band is covered automatically by multilevel sampling.
- **FFT auto-estimation** matches manual tuning (0% error); ±20–30% noise barely affects accuracy; **frozen** multilevel sampling beats or matches learnable frequencies.
