# E16 — Frequency Layering Strategy Ablation

**Purpose.** Quantify the impact of the multi-scale three-level frequency initialization versus simpler alternatives across all 9 benchmark cases, with everything else fixed to the E11 settings.

**Reviewer comments addressed.** R7.2, R3.5.

## Setup

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Scale | 9 cases × 5 strategies × 3 seeds (135 per-run JSON files) |
| Strategies | `default` (3-level, = E11, reused not rerun), `S1_single`, `S2_two`, `S4_continuous`, `S5_40_40_20` |
| Config | Same NUM_MODES, NUM_FREQ, `w_char`, 10,000 epochs, lr, collocation, and params as E11 |

## Conclusions

- The three-level `default` is the only strategy with **zero** catastrophic failures; its worst-case L2 (~0.03) is ~3× better than the next-best and ~30×+ better than single/continuous.
- It is strictly optimal on the hard cases C3 (nonlinear) and C5 (obstacle scattering).
- Single-frequency wins on pure single-frequency MMS cases (C2/C4/C6/C7) but collapses to 100% error when the spectrum broadens — an "oracle" advantage, not a robust default.
- On smooth flows (C8/C9), all strategies are within statistical noise.
