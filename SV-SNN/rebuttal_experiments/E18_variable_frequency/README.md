# E18 — Variable-Frequency (Chirp) Fair Comparison

**Purpose.** Test adaptivity when the local wavenumber varies spatially (a chirp manufactured solution), sweeping the chirp ratio from mild to strong.

**Reviewer comments addressed.** Concern about methods when the local wavenumber varies with space (variable frequency / chirp).

## Setup

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| PDE | Poisson `-Δu = f` on `[0,1]²` |
| Chirp | `φ(x) = k₀x(1+cx)`, `u = sin(φ(x))sin(φ(y))`; `k₀ = 6π`, ratio `r = k_max/k_min = 1+2c` |
| `r` scan | 1, 2, 4, 8 (`k_max = w_char`: 6π, 12π, 24π, 48π) |
| Methods | SV-SNN, SPINN, FourierPINN, SIREN, PINN |
| Seeds | 3 (60 runs) |

## Key results

Best relative L2 (mean ± std):

| `r` | **SV-SNN** | SPINN | FourierPINN | SIREN | PINN |
|----:|-----------:|------:|------------:|------:|-----:|
| 1 | **0.034 ± 0.014** | 0.145 ± 0.023 | 0.499 ± 0.349 | 0.235 ± 0.048 | 2.26 ± 0.45 |
| 2 | 0.021 ± 0.022 | **0.013 ± 0.010** | 0.232 ± 0.166 | 0.170 ± 0.051 | 2.65 ± 0.63 |
| 4 | 0.033 ± 0.009 | **0.025 ± 0.022** | 0.177 ± 0.040 | 0.491 ± 0.352 | 2.03 ± 0.58 |
| 8 | 0.487 ± 0.046 | **0.086 ± 0.047** | 0.999 ± 0.001 | 0.891 ± 0.153 | 10.04 ± 0.26 |
| params | 1170 | 1164 | 1249 | 1143 | 1217 |

## Conclusions

- SV-SNN is best at single-frequency / weak chirp (`r = 1`).
- At moderate chirp (`r = 2, 4`), SV-SNN and SPINN tie (~0.01–0.03), both far ahead of the Fourier-MLP methods.
- At strong chirp (`r = 8`), SPINN beats SV-SNN (0.086 vs 0.487): the **frozen** SV-SNN spectrum is a stated limitation for strongly space-varying wavenumbers.
