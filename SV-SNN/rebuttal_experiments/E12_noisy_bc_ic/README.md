# E12 — Noisy Boundary Conditions

**Purpose.** Test robustness when boundary supervision is corrupted by measurement noise while the PDE residual remains clean — a proxy for noisy boundary/initial conditions in practice.

**Reviewer comments addressed.** R1.4 (robustness to noisy BC/IC).

## Setup

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| PDE | 2D separable Helmholtz `-Δu - κ²u = f`, `u = sin(κx)sin(κy)`, κ = 24π, homogeneous Dirichlet |
| Noise | ε ∈ {0, 1%, 5%, 10%} injected into boundary values relative to interior RMS (~0.5) |
| Methods | SV-SNN (1170 params) vs FourierPINN (49729) vs PINN (50049) |
| Training | 8000 epochs, Adam (lr = 1e-3) |
| Seeds | 3 |

## Key results

Best relative L2 (mean ± std):

| Noise ε | **SV-SNN** | FourierPINN | PINN |
|---------|-----------:|------------:|-----:|
| 0% | **0.0421 ± 0.0056** | 0.329 ± 0.049 | 0.997 ± 0.002 |
| 1% | **0.0417 ± 0.0091** | 0.330 ± 0.048 | 0.998 ± 0.003 |
| 5% | **0.0381 ± 0.0086** | 0.330 ± 0.048 | 0.996 ± 0.004 |
| 10% | **0.0325 ± 0.0093** | 0.329 ± 0.048 | 0.998 ± 0.002 |

Training time: SV-SNN ~10.4 s, FourierPINN ~11 s, PINN ~14 s.

## Conclusions

- SV-SNN is ~8× better than FourierPINN and ~24× better than PINN at all noise levels, with ~1/42 the parameters.
- SV-SNN's L2 does not degrade from ε = 0 to 10% (0.042 → 0.033, within seed variance); the frozen spectral basis acts as an implicit regularizer.
- All methods are insensitive at this noise level because the physics residual dominates the total loss. (The BC→IC analogy: the same mechanism applies to noisy initial conditions in time-dependent problems.)
