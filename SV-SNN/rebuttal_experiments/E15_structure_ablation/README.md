# E15 — SV-SNN Structure Ablation

**Purpose.** Disentangle whether SV-SNN's gains come from (a) variable separation, (b) the Fourier spectral basis, or (c) the characteristic-frequency scale `w_char` — rather than from more parameters or better tuning. Two axes are studied at the matched parameter budget.

**Reviewer comments addressed.** R7.5, R4.2, R10.1, R1.5, R3.2, R5.1.

## Setup

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Axis A (structure) | E11 matched baselines reinterpreted as controlled ablations at ±10% SV-SNN params: SV-SNN full vs FourierPINN (Fourier, no separation) vs SPINN (separation, no Fourier) vs PINN (neither) |
| Axis B (`w_char` scale) | scale all frozen frequencies by s ∈ {0.6, 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.5}; s = 1.0 reuses E11 |
| Scale | 9 cases × 5 scales × 3 seeds (135 per-run JSON files) |

## Conclusions

- Full SV-SNN (separation × Fourier) is optimal on **9/9** cases at the matched budget; either component alone fails on subsets of cases.
- `w_char` sensitivity is U-shaped: ×1.0 is optimal on 7/9 cases; underestimation (×0.6) is catastrophic on the high-frequency cases.
- Axis A uses E11 matched baselines as proxies (the hard-switch ablation is E3); C7's `w_char` valley is flat near ×0.8, and C8/C9 are nearly insensitive to scale.
