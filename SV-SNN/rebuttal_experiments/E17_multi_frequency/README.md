# E17 — Multi-Frequency Fair Comparison

**Purpose.** Evaluate SV-SNN against baselines when the solution contains multiple distinct frequency components, under a strict equal-parameter budget.

**Reviewer comments addressed.** Concern about performance when the solution has multiple distinct frequency components.

## Setup

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| PDE | Poisson `-Δu = f` on `[0,1]²`, Dirichlet BC from the exact solution |
| MMS | `u = Σⱼ aⱼ sin(kⱼx)sin(kⱼy)`, `aⱼ = 1`; spectra of 2/3/4 components with `w_char = k_max` |
| Methods | SV-SNN, FourierPINN, SIREN, SPINN, PINN (no prior) |
| Fairness | Same multi-level frequency sampler and `w_char` for prior-based methods; params matched to SV-SNN ±10% |
| Seeds | 3 (45 runs: 3 spectra × 5 methods × 3 seeds) |

## Key results

Best relative L2 (mean ± std):

| Spectrum | **SV-SNN** | FourierPINN | SIREN | SPINN | PINN |
|----------|-----------:|------------:|------:|------:|-----:|
| 2 components | **0.077 ± 0.024** | 0.507 ± 0.162 | 0.693 ± 0.015 | 0.708 ± 0.001 | 6.38 ± 2.55 |
| 3 components | **0.029 ± 0.016** | 0.678 ± 0.140 | 0.609 ± 0.021 | 0.586 ± 0.003 | 6.64 ± 0.61 |
| 4 components | **0.682 ± 0.012** | 0.821 ± 0.131 | 0.824 ± 0.124 | 0.768 ± 0.072 | 6.04 ± 1.26 |
| params | 1170 | 1249 | 1143 | 1164 | 1217 |

Per-component error (4-component, mean): SV-SNN captures `k = 24π, 36π` at ~5e-4–8e-4 but underfits `k = 6π, 14π` (~0.95).

## Conclusions

- SV-SNN is best at all three spectral-richness levels: ~6.6× ahead of the best baseline at 2 components (0.077 vs FourierPINN 0.507) and ~20× ahead at 3 components (0.029 vs SPINN 0.586).
- At 4 components (≈6× bandwidth span on only ~1170 params), all methods struggle; the margin shrinks to ~1.1× (SV-SNN 0.682 vs SPINN 0.768), with SV-SNN prioritizing the high bands and still attaining the lowest overall L2.
- The prior-free PINN fails (>5) on all spectra.
